> ## Issue #72 (text from Hanif)
> Title: A correct part placed before its task counts as a mistake
> 
> User story (PLAN.md row 5): As a learner, in any project I want to build like a game: a level map I move along, and inside each level one task at a time, with a pop-up on the 3D board showing where the part or wire goes and explaining why, the target pin glowing, a snap or buzz when I place a wire, and XP for each task, so that I learn the reason behind every connection.
> 
> Problem: A part that does a later task of the level buzzes and counts as a mistake, although its pins are correct. Example: in Perch Level 3, the pop-up asks for WS → GPIO 4. The learner wires SD → GPIO 6 first (task 7). The wire buzzes, the Mistake pop-up opens, and `mistakes` goes up by 1. This takes away the no-mistake bonus and adds a bar to the dashboard mistakes chart. Cause: `step()` in `lib/game/engine.ts` compares a new part only with the tasks up to `reached`. A part that does a task after `reached` is not found, so it is a mistake. Found during #63 ("A correct part placed out of order still counts as a mistake; this fix does not change that (a separate issue)").
> 
> Decision (Hanif): (a) Quiet snap. A correct part placed early snaps in with no mistake and no note. Its task ticks when the learner gets to it.
> 
> What to do: (1) In `step()`, when a new part does a task after `reached` and makes no forbidden connection: snap, no Mistake, no change to `mistakes`. The pop-up stays on the task of now. (2) When the learner gets to that task, `advance()` finishes it by itself (this already works for tasks that the board does), and its XP counts one time only.
> 
> Acceptance criteria:
> - A part that does a later task gives a snap, not a buzz. `mistakes` does not change. No Mistake pop-up opens.
> - The pop-up and the task list stay on the task of now.
> - When the learner gets to the early task, it finishes by itself. `tasksDone` and `xp` count it one time only.
> - A part that does no task is still a mistake (`wrong_wire`, `wrong_part`, `wrong_place`).
> - A part that makes a forbidden connection (for example 3V3 to GND) is still a mistake, even if it also does a later task.
> - Undo or Remove of the early part does not give a Mistake, and the task is not done any more.
> - Tests first in `tests/engine.test.ts` (red, then green).
> - No migration, no content change, and no change to the saved-board format.
> - Type check, lint and all Vitest tests pass.
> Not in this issue: a note such as "Good, but do this one first" (option b); changes to the level Check.
> 
> Watch for: tasks that need "Got it" (`needsAck`), the button/resistor side choice (`swaps`) — an early part must not lock a wrong side for an earlier task — and `taskMarks()`, which must not tick a task beyond `reached` early (the tick comes when the learner gets there). Check the ready-made boards and Arduino Foundations levels too (`tests/arduino.test.ts`).
