> ## Issue #73 (text from Hanif)
> Title: The same level open in two tabs saves over the newer board
> 
> User story (PLAN.md row 5): As a learner, in any project I want to build like a game: a level map I move along, and inside each level one task at a time, with a pop-up on the 3D board showing where the part or wire goes and explaining why, the target pin glowing, a snap or buzz when I place a wire, and XP for each task, so that I learn the reason behind every connection.
> 
> Problem: When one user has the same level open in two tabs, the work of one tab can go away. Example: the learner opens Perch Level 2 in tab A and tab B. The learner puts 3 wires in tab A. Then the learner changes the board in tab B. Tab B writes its older board to the same browser entry (`jumper:<user>:perch/2`). After a reload, the 3 wires of tab A are gone. Cause: each tab keeps its board in memory, and `components/build/autosave.ts` writes it to localStorage. The tab that saves last wins. No tab listens for a save from another tab. Found during #64 ("The case 'same level open in two tabs of one user' is not in this issue").
> 
> What to do: (1) Listen for the browser `storage` event in build mode. (2) When another tab saves the same key (same user, project and level), this tab does not write its older board over the newer one. It loads the newer board and progress, or it shows a note "This level is open in another tab" and stops saving until a reload. (3) Put the decision logic ("is this save from another tab, and is it for my level?") in a small pure function, with unit tests.
> 
> Acceptance criteria:
> - After a save in tab A, a change in tab B does not remove the work of tab A from the browser save.
> - Tab B shows the board of tab A, or the note that the level is open in another tab.
> - Another level, another project or another user in the other tab changes nothing in this tab.
> - The note text is in `content/ui/build.json`.
> - Unit tests first (red, then green) for the pure function.
> - A browser check with two tabs (see below).
> - No migration, no content change to levels or tasks, and no change to the saved-board format.
> - Type check, lint and all Vitest tests pass.
> Not in this issue: two devices at the same time; sessions in the database.
> 
> Choice: pick the simpler of "load the newer board" and "show the note and stop saving". My recommendation is the note and stop saving (no merge of two boards, no surprise jump of the 3D board, and session counters stay with one tab), but if loading is clearly simpler and safe with the engine progress and the session id, explain why in the Claude notes and the PR body.
